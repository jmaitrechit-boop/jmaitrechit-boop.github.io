Back

Throughout my life I’ve used various forms of image making, whether it be through taking photos of my many pets or creating an animation on my computer. It’s been a source of joy ever since the quarantine in 2020 where I first learned how to do animations on my computer to tell stories. Even though I never really showed it to anyone else other than my friend group, during that time it was thrilling being able to create something that was my own project for the first time. It was until late high school where I utilized my animation skills to create a game for my final AP Computer Science project, a project I was incredibly proud of. Being able to create these animations allowed me to create the game, which in turn, created an experience I can physically open up and play again. I also frequently share photos of my pets on social media, and it’s allowed me to look back and see how much they’ve grown. This has been extremely helpful as of late due to the recent passing of my dog. Looking at photos and videos of him allowed me to revisit these memories and come to terms with the fact that he lived a happy and fulfilling life, and that I should be happy he’s in a better place now as well. Choosing to take a picture is somewhat hard for me to do, as I don’t particularly take pictures of myself. However, I do generally enjoy taking pictures of my friends since the photo encapsulates what I saw, since I was the one who took the photo from my point of view rather than a solo or group selfie. Just looking at pictures like these makes me feel the same happiness I felt in that moment. This is generally the same for videos, where I like to focus on things that aren’t myself most of the time, such as a concert I go to, or a funny moment that happens with my friends. One of these moments was a Porter Robinson concert I went to back in 2024 and it was also one of my first concert experiences. I still have many of the videos I took that day and look fondly on them. Being able to look back at these videos not only brings up nostalgia for me, but I can even share that feeling with other people like my friends who were also there. Reminiscing about the concert inspires us to also make things of our own such as my animations or my friends music. Overall, digital media has been a deeply impactful tool the entirety of my life as the many things that I’ve created or experienced exist on my device which I can view at any time. The feeling when revisiting these moments inspire me to keep looking for more opportunities like concerts to go to, or to continue developing small projects in my own time, purely for my own or my friends' entertainment.
