Creative Statement:

My name is James Maitrechit, a second year student at Hunter College and I like to create digital art such as animations as well as create games. I particularly enjoy creating action animations that have a story, as well as writing lore pertaining to these stories. My main insipration for these animations are old pivot animations from creators on youtube such as TheBigWhiteGun which involve stick figures fighting in various stylized animations. I've created games in my senior year of high school which have been inspired by turn based games such as Library of Ruina and Limbus Company that integrate with my animation ability as I can give a visual of what the player is doing.

I enjoy working with friends who have unique skills compared to mine, such as creating music or video editing as these are areas that I am lacking in and collaborating with others allow me to expand the scope of my animations by a lot. I have been creating this one world that I really want to animate, but I don't have the time or skills at the moment to do so. As time goes on, I'd like to finish writing and pre production on it and finally get to animate it one day.
